# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone involved in this project.

## Respectful Behavior

All participants must treat others with respect and professionalism. Healthy discussion, helpful feedback, and polite communication are expected at all times.

## No Harassment

Harassment, abuse, discrimination, intimidation, or offensive behavior of any kind will not be tolerated.

## Inclusive Community

This project aims to be open and inclusive to people of all backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Reporting Process

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it by contacting:
mrspaceresearcher@gmail.com

Please include:
- A clear description of the incident.
- Names of people involved, if known.
- Date, time, and context.
- Any screenshots or supporting details.

## Community Rules

- Be kind and respectful.
- Do not use abusive, hateful, or discriminatory language.
- Stay focused on the project and keep discussions constructive.
- Respect the opinions and work of others.
- Do not spam, troll, or intentionally disrupt the community.

## Enforcement

Anyone asked to stop harmful behavior is expected to comply immediately. Repeated or severe violations may result in removal from project participation.

Thank you for helping maintain a positive and respectful community.
